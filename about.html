Hey, I'm Priyanka.

I'm a designer that likes solving hard problems and navigating through ambiguity.

Currently, I lead 0→1 design at Memmy. I'm also building Manila Folder.

Previously, I've crafted web experiences for the global design community at Kaikasen, led design at EvoLab, and designed the internship platform for Kultivate Labs.

Where I've lived has shaped me into the person that I am today.

Born in Seattle, I spent my early childhood in Germany, lived in Kansas, and finally ended up in California — where I'm proud to call Northern California home.

I also grew up visiting family in Manila, so I consider this home too :)

Beyond design, you can find me finding peace in nature, capturing the world through my lens, and brewing the perfect cup of matcha.

Let's stay in touch.

I'm always open to new opportunities, collaborations, or just a friendly chat about design.
